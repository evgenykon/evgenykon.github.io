A browser-based open-map game (MapLibre + OpenStreetMap) where the player must reach a shelter before an explosion occurs at a random epicenter.

Choose difficulty at the start — it affects the timer and blast strength. The game requests geolocation, fetches roads and buildings from the Overpass API within ~15 km radius, builds a road graph, and scatters 15–20 shelters in a 5–10 km ring from the epicenter.

Controls: WASD — movement, mouse — camera rotation, E — get in/out of the car. The car snaps to the road graph and drives on real OSM roads.

At the moment of the explosion: if inside a shelter — win, otherwise — lose.

Stack: TypeScript, Vite 5, Vue 3 (Composition API), Pinia, MapLibre GL JS, Turf.js, Docker.
