**NukeOpp** — a personal manifesto against collective insanity. The site is dedicated to the nuclear threat: scenarios, weapons, survival information.

**Try it live:** [evgenykon.github.io/nukeopp](https://evgenykon.github.io/nukeopp/)

![ui](/blog-photos/SCR-20260530-nisu.png)

### About

Human history has approached a line beyond which history itself may cease to exist. The nuclear threat is not a ghost from Cold War textbooks — it is a reality that grows and mutates right now.

This project is an attempt to bring sobriety back to public discourse. We have created a force surpassing our collective reason and entrusted it to the ambitions of a few.

Nuclear weapons are the greatest existential challenge of our time. Let this project be our call to action: to learn, to remember, and to resist the destruction of our common home.
